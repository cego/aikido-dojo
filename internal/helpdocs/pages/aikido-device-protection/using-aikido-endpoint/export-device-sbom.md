# Export Device SBOM

Aikido lets you export a CSV of all packages detected across your managed devices. That list is your device SBOM (software bill of materials, the inventory of packages in use). You can export the full fleet inventory, or narrow it down using filters before downloading.

## How to export

1. Go to [Device Protection and open the Packages tab](https://app.aikido.dev/endpoint-protection/packages).
2. Optionally apply filters to narrow the export, for example by ecosystem or by a specific device.
3. Click **Actions** in the top right, then select **Download CSV**.

The CSV reflects exactly what is shown on the page, so any active filters are applied to the export.
