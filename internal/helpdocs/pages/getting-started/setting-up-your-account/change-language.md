# Change Language

Set the language of the Aikido app from your profile. The setting applies to your user only and doesn't change the language for other members of your workspace.

Supported languages: English, Japanese, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese.

## Change your language

1. Go to [**My Profile**](https://app.aikido.dev/my-profile?regionRedirect=1) via your avatar in the top right corner of the Aikido app.
2. In **Personal Profile**, open the **Language** dropdown.
3. Select your language.
